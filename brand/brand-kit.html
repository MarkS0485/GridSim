GridSim

A visual identity for the GridSim account — drawn from the product itself: the control-room canvas, the electric-blue frequency trace, and the nadir that holds above the floor. Not the TSGB red-white-blue; GridSim's own palette.

01 — Profile avatar · two routes

The mark

X crops avatars to a circle and renders them as small as 48 px, so the mark has to survive both. Both routes are built on the deep canvas and stay legible when tiny. Pick one as the account's face.

Concept A — “Nadir”

The signature frequency dip holding above the amber LFDD floor. The product’s thesis in one glyph; reads as a live pulse at small sizes.

recommended

Concept B — “Node”

A compact tube-map network with a green slack bus. Leans on the schematic side of GridSim; more literal “grid”, busier at 48 px.

alt

02 — Header · 1500 × 500

The banner

Full-bleed control-room strip: the wordmark and positioning line held in the center-safe band, a faint network across the width, and the live trace sweeping to a nadir that holds above the floor. The bottom-left avatar overlap and the mobile side-crop are kept clear.

amber = LFDD floor · green node = nadir held above it · avatar sits bottom-left (kept clear)

03 — Wordmark · lockups

Wordmark

Grid in text-white, Sim in electric blue — the split marks the two halves of the product (the network, and the simulation over it). Works on dark and light.
