$ gridsim interpose
usage: gridsim interpose [case] --protocol <name> (--listen host:port | --port N) --crosswalk <map.json>
                         [--measurements-dir <dir>] [--out-store <dir>] [--source-id <id>] [--window-ms N]
                         [--monitor] [--ticks N] [--live-seconds S] [--tolerate-malformed]
       gridsim interpose --list-protocols
Receives live RTU/SCADA frames, corrects & decodes them, and writes gridsim-measurements/1 windows.
Live frames feed the estimator only after ageing past the 169 h horizon (monitor-only until then).
[exit 2]
