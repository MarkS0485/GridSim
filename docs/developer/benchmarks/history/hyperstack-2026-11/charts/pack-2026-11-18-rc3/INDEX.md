# GridSim benchmark chart pack (2026-11-18)

Source: box 2 (hyperstack-2-canada, EPYC 7763, 28 vCPU) `suite-full-20261117T2356Z/comparison.csv`, box 1 (hyperstack-1-norway, EPYC 7452, 4 vCPU) `suite-full-20261117T2159Z/comparison.csv` for chart a6 only. Generated by `charts.py` (python charts.py). Method: one core, Release, tiered JIT off, mean of 10 timed solves, solve time only, flat start, 67 MATPOWER 8.1 cases. Accuracy gate: status complete, converged 10, max residual <= 1e-8 pu. Win/tie/loss: sparse vs fastest accurate other solver, 10% tie band (matches `summarize.py`: 54 / 3 / 5 / 5 on both boxes).

Every PNG carries a footer with hardware, method and data source. Headline charts 1600x900; `-square` variants 1080x1080.

## Notes on honesty

- Headline speedup, time-saved and capacity charts state their range in the subtitle (<=1,400 buses, or the four named example cases). The scorecard and analysis charts show all losses.
- Losses (box 2): case_ACTIVSg2000 (4.57x slower than pandapower); case2848rte (GridSim no result, matpower passed); case3012wp (3.49x slower than powsybl); case3120sp (1.70x slower than powsybl); case3375wp (2.88x slower than powsybl).
- Ties: case2383wp, case2737sop, case2746wp. Nobody solved: case16am, case_RTS_GMLC, case1888rte, case1951rte, case2868rte (case_RTS_GMLC was not materialised for any solver: unsupported dcline field).
- Per-solver palette: skill reference slots in fixed order (GridSim blue first); adjacent-pair validation passes (validate_palette.py, light mode); the all-pairs check fails beyond 3 slots, so marker shapes, a legend and fixed order carry identity on the multi-solver charts.
